# Image generation prompts — Jewoulingo case illustrations

**Tool:** GPT Image (or Gemini 2.5 Flash Image / "nano banana").
**Aspect ratio:** 3:2 landscape (≈1536×1024). Keep a clean margin for the text.

When you get each image back, save it with the filename noted under each prompt
and drop them in the repo root next to `demo.html` — I'll wire them into the cards.

---

## SHARED STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every prompt)

> Warm, friendly flat-illustration style with soft parchment/cream background
> (#f7f1e4) and gold (#c9a227) accents — like a polished educational app
> illustration, not a cartoon. Clean vector shapes, gentle shadows, rounded
> friendly faces. All human characters are observant Jewish men: each wears a
> **black velvet yarmulke (kippah)**, a **dark suit jacket over a white collared
> shirt**, neatly trimmed beard, warm respectful expressions. Tasteful, dignified,
> non-caricatured. Include the specified **English label text rendered cleanly
> and legibly** inside the image (sans-serif, dark ink color). Leave generous
> breathing room around the text so nothing is cut off.

## 1. Case A — "The Seller's Claim" (Bava Kamma 75b)

**Filename to save as:** `img-caseA.png`

> [SHARED STYLE BLOCK] —
> Scene: two Jewish men in yarmulkes and dark suit jackets standing on opposite
> sides of a small green field with a wooden fence and a deed/contract scroll in
> the center between them. The man on the LEFT (Reuven, the seller) gestures
> toward the field with one open hand and holds the rolled deed in the other,
> looking earnest. The man on the RIGHT (Shimon, the buyer) has arms crossed,
> looking skeptical. A coin pouch sits on the fence post between them to show
> money already changed hands.
> Top banner text: "CASE A · The Seller's Claim".
> Small caption ribbon under each figure: left figure labeled "REUVEN — 'the
> sale was only conditional'", right figure labeled "SHIMON — 'no such condition'".
> Bottom strip text: "Money already paid · no witnesses · who do we believe?".

## 2. Case B — "The Conditional Get" (Gittin 34a)

**Filename to save as:** `img-caseB.png`

> [SHARED STYLE BLOCK] —
> Scene: three figures. On the LEFT, a Jewish husband in a yarmulke and dark suit
> jacket hands a rolled document (a "get" / divorce document) to a MESSENGER in
> the CENTER — the messenger is also a Jewish man in a yarmulke and jacket,
> mid-stride carrying the scroll. On the RIGHT, the wife (modestly dressed with a
> head covering) receives the document. Above the husband, a small thought/speech
> bubble reads "I cancelled it!"; above the wife a speech bubble reads "I heard
> nothing." A faint dotted arrow shows the document traveling left-to-right from
> husband to messenger to wife.
> Top banner text: "CASE B · The Conditional Get".
> Bottom strip text: "He admits sending it · claims he cancelled it · is she free
> to remarry?".

## 3. The concept — "Miggo: the stronger claim not taken"

**Filename to save as:** `img-miggo-concept.png`

> [SHARED STYLE BLOCK] —
> A clean explanatory diagram, NOT a scene. A single Jewish man in a yarmulke and
> dark suit jacket stands in the center. Above him, two speech paths branch
> upward: the LEFT path is a large bold bubble labeled "STRONGER CLAIM" with a
> faded/greyed-out X over it (the claim he could have made but didn't); the RIGHT
> path is a smaller highlighted gold bubble labeled "WEAKER CLAIM" with a glowing
> checkmark (the claim he actually made). A big gold arrow curves from the unused
> stronger claim down to the man, labeled "מִגּוֹ / MIGGO". Bottom banner text:
> "Since he could have said something stronger — believe the weaker claim."
> Keep it diagrammatic, balanced, and uncluttered.
